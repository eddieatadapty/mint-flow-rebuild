Why you’re seeing this

30,000 subscription apps taught us what moves conversion. Here’s what we’d test first at Mint.

Adapty runs subscriptions for more than 30,000 apps, so we see which onboarding and paywall changes lift revenue across the whole market, not just inside one app.

We pointed that at Mint. We took your own screens, copy and prices from your India App Store listing, its screenshots and in-app purchases, and your Meta ads (we have no recording of the app, so every screen is a proposal in your design), and rebuilt the path from install to paywall around the five ideas with the most potential.
